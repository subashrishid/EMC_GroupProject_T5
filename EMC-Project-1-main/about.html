FAQ

Common questions

Do you offer fixed-price contracts?

Yes. After design approval you receive a detailed fixed-price quote with a payment schedule tied to milestones.

How long does a typical house take?

A 2,000 sq ft independent house usually takes 8 to 10 months from foundation to handover.

Is there a warranty?

All structural work carries a 5-year warranty, and we provide a free inspection after handover.

Which areas do you serve?

Chennai and across Tamil Nadu, with projects also taken up in neighbouring states.
